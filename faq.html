Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about renting with RentEase.

How does monthly rental work?

You choose an item, pick a rental period of 3, 6 or 12 months, and pay the monthly rent. You can keep the item for the whole period and return it at the end.

Why is a longer rental period cheaper?

Longer plans give you a lower monthly rent. A 6-month plan gets a small discount, and a 12-month plan gets a bigger one.

What is the security deposit?

The deposit is a one-time amount paid when you start a rental. We hold it during your rental period and return it when the item comes back in good condition.

Who pays for repairs?

Normal wear and tear is covered during your rental. You can send a maintenance request from My Rentals, and our team will arrange the repair.

Can I move to a new city?

Yes. You can ask us to collect the item at your old address and deliver it to your new one. Pickup and delivery depend on your location.

How do I return an item?

Schedule a pickup from My Rentals when your rental period ends. We will collect the item from your address.

Can I check if you deliver to my area?

Yes. Enter your pincode at checkout to see if delivery is available in your area.

Can I upgrade or change an item later?

Yes. When a rental ends, you can choose a new item or a different plan for the same address.
